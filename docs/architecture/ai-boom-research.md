# AI boom opening and model research

New World 3 campaigns open in 2023 Q1, the first full quarter after ChatGPT's public launch. This is an open-ended, historically inspired game scenario, not a historical economic dataset. The AI frontier starts with lower capability, benchmark saturation and synthetic-data maturity. AI catalogue end demand and seeded AI revenue targets are 15% of the former 2027 calibration; opening prices, production capacity and funding are remeasured by the existing economy builder.

Advanced agent harnesses, broad AI suites, humanoids and AI wearables remain research opportunities. They have no opening commercial ownership. Early backgrounds use vertical AI pilots, industrial arms and conventional devices instead. The early copilot framework supplies applications. Background-card figures are measured against all 90 background/region combinations.

`aiModels.ts` defines 32 supported combinations of architecture, training method and output, plus five specialised datasets. Dense Transformers, mixture-of-experts, state-space models and diffusion networks have distinct training and serving factors. Pretraining, instruction tuning, human-feedback training and distillation have different prerequisites, compute/data needs and programme costs. Supported outputs are text, code, images, speech, video and structured actions. Compatibility here is the game's supported catalogue, not a claim that other research combinations are physically impossible.

Each design is an economic node and a real paid research programme. Pretraining requires training infrastructure and the appropriate corpus. Subsequent methods require the base design; human-feedback training additionally consumes preference data. Distillation lowers serving requirements and the capability factor. These game-balance factors affect production quality and costs; they are not measured real-world benchmarks. Text-token APIs accept language/code designs, not visual generators. Designs can be sold as model licences into their respective customer markets.

The research panel and node drawer use the same catalogue as the simulation. Undiscovered designs cannot be imported through the generic market. A later design needs the buyer's own active production line or a supplier whose published terms admit that buyer. Merely owning a private research result does not make it available to other companies. The player validator, NPC supplier selection, route previews and production resolver enforce the same rule.

The ycvps reset is an explicit operator action requested by the owner, not an automatic save migration. Back up the canonical games and cloud saves, replace the affected records with quarter-zero checkpoints using their existing owner/run identities, increment canonical revisions, and clear old queues and resolution receipts. Update the matching cloud autosaves. Existing browser revisions then become stale and reload from the canonical file. Keep Codex credentials and application configuration intact.
